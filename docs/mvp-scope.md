# MVP Mission

###### Enable members of the ArtCraft community to list their projects, identify the people involved, and spot opportunities for collaboration or alignment.

1. A central project registry
Each project has a name, description, objective, status, and relevant links. Ideas without a GitHub repository are also accepted.

2. Identifiable participants
Each entry indicates who proposed the project, who is actively contributing, and who simply wishes to follow it.

3. Visible similar projects
A "Related projects" section helps spot projects addressing a similar need and open their details to compare their goals.

6. Key statuses

	**Proposed**
	The idea exists, but development hasn't necessarily started.

	**Seeking collaborators**
	The project is looking for people to make progress.

	In development
	Development is underway.

	**Completed**
	The project is considered finished.

	**Inactive**
	The project is currently no longer active.



5. Required fields



Name and description        Understand the project
Objective                   Compare projects on substance
Status                      Know where it stands
Creator or lead             Know who to contact
Participants                Understand who is involved
Links                       Access the repository or discussions
Related projects            Identify overlaps





###### Simple reporting

Allow reporting broken links, misleading projects, or potentially malicious content. Reporting does not prove that content is malicious; it triggers a review.



###### An identifiable source

Official repository or link provided by the creator, with a clear distinction between verified projects and simply referenced projects.



###### Links to plugins

Provide the ability to reference plugins in the catalog, without implementing their download for now.
