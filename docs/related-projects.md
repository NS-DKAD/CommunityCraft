# Substance 3D/Painter



### SubCraft by MistyMystery

##### Goal

An independent, open-source 3D texture painting application in the spirit of Substance Painter, written in Rust. Paint directly on 3D models with layered PBR materials, masks, generators and smart materials, bake mesh maps, and export game-ready textures.
